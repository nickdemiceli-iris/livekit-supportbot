# Simple Loans Support Knowledge Base

## Identity and account verification

- Before discussing account-specific details, confirm the customer full name and one unique identifier (phone number, email, or account number).
- Do not disclose balances, payment schedules, or contract terms to third parties.
- If the caller cannot verify identity, provide only general policy information and offer a callback once verification is completed.

## Loan status and disbursement questions

- Standard disbursement timeline is same day to one business day after final approval and required documents are complete.
- Disbursement timing may be delayed by bank holidays, incorrect bank details, or pending fraud-review checks.
- Customers can check status by contacting support with their account identifier.
- Support agents must never promise guaranteed same-day funding.

## Payment and due-date policy

- Monthly payments are due on the date listed in the customer agreement.
- If a payment date falls on a bank holiday, processing is handled on the next business day.
- There is no prepayment penalty, so customers may pay early without extra fees.
- Partial payments may reduce the outstanding principal but do not automatically change the next due date unless the servicing team confirms a modified schedule.

## Late payment and hardship

- If a customer expects a late payment, direct them to contact support before the due date whenever possible.
- Hardship review may be available on a case-by-case basis.
- Support can create a hardship escalation ticket; approval is not guaranteed.

## Voluntary Debt Cancellation (VDC)

- VDC is an optional protection product and is not required for every customer.
- If active on an account, VDC charges are included in the disclosed payment estimate.
- Customers can request a review of VDC enrollment and eligibility by support escalation.

## Vehicle and title documentation

- Required documentation typically includes a valid government ID, vehicle title information, and proof of address.
- If title ownership is not free and clear, the customer may still be reviewed, but additional lienholder details are required.
- A virtual vehicle inspection is often required before final disbursement.

## Virtual inspection troubleshooting

- If camera access fails in the inspection link, ask the customer to close and reopen the browser, then retry with camera permissions enabled.
- If the inspection upload stalls, recommend switching from cellular to stable Wi-Fi and retrying.
- If inspection still fails after basic troubleshooting, escalate to a human inspection specialist.

## Login and portal support

- If a customer cannot log in, first confirm they are using the correct email.
- Password reset emails may take up to 5 minutes; ask the customer to check spam or junk folders.
- If reset email does not arrive after 10 minutes, escalate to account support for manual reset review.

## Escalation criteria

- Escalate immediately for potential fraud, legal threats, identity mismatch, or repeated failed payment issues.
- Escalate when policy exceptions are requested and not explicitly covered in the knowledge base.
- Escalate when a customer requests a supervisor or human specialist.

## Communication standards

- Keep responses concise and clear.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Confirm resolution before ending the conversation.
- If unresolved, offer follow-up time and preferred contact channel.
